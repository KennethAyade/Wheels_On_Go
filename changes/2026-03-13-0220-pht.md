## Phase A: Static Payment Gateway

### A1. Payment Module (Backend)
**New files:**
- `apps/api/src/payment/payment.module.ts` — NestJS module
- `apps/api/src/payment/payment.service.ts` — Core payment logic
- `apps/api/src/payment/payment.controller.ts` — `POST /rides/:id/confirm-cash-payment` (DRIVER only)

**Logic:**
- GCash/Card rides auto-complete payment on ride completion via `processPayment()`
- Cash rides stay PENDING until driver calls `confirmCashPayment()`
- `completePayment()` runs in Prisma `$transaction`: updates ride paymentStatus, creates Transaction record, upserts DriverWallet with net earnings
- Commission: `grossAmount * commissionRate` (default 20%), net = gross - commission
- Payment references: `SIM-{timestamp}` for digital, `CASH-{timestamp}` for cash
- Payment gateway: `SIMULATED` for digital, `CASH` for cash
